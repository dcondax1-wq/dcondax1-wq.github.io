Big widget

Half your Home Screen: live numbers + 8 buttons

FREE

Set it up (2 minutes)

  1. Get Scriptable (free). Open the App Store
  2. One tap:
    It copies the script and opens a new blank script. Long-press, Paste, tap the title, name it Olympus, Done.
  3. Add it: long-press the Home Screen, tap + (top left), search Scriptable, swipe to Large (half the screen) or Medium, Add Widget.
  4. Long-press the widget, Edit Widget, Script = Olympus. Done.

Already have the old Olympus script? Replace its text with the new copy: the widget picks it up by itself.

What you get

Top: PC status and today's $ (tap = Board). One line per account: balance, today $, drawdown room left, and LONG / SHORT +$ when a trade is open (tap = NQ bot). Large adds Kalshi and the NQ price.

Buttons (Large = 8, Medium = 4: NQ bot, Kalshi, Jarvis, Decide): each opens its page.

Bottom: where the numbers came from (LIVE · PC, PC · OLD, PAPER · SITE, CACHED) and their age.

Honest note on refresh: iOS decides when widgets redraw, usually every 5-15 min (it asks every 15 min). Numbers are never newer than the PC's last 15-min post: check the age at the bottom. Buttons open Safari or your Home Screen app. Read only: it sends nothing and cannot arm or place anything.

No Scriptable? Shortcuts widget

The built-in Shortcuts app can show 8 buttons (no numbers). For each line below: Shortcuts, +, add action Open URLs, paste the URL, name the shortcut. Then long-press the Home Screen, +, Shortcuts, Large (8 buttons), Add Widget.
