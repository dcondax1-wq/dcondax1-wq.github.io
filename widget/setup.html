NQ NOW widget

The NQ bots on your Home Screen and lock screen

FREE

NQ NOW: one paste (2 minutes)

  1. Get Scriptable (free). Open the App Store
  2. One tap:
    Long-press, Paste, tap the title, name it NQ NOW, Done.
  3. Home Screen: long-press it, +, Scriptable, pick Small, Medium or Large, Add Widget. Long-press the widget, Edit Widget, Script = NQ NOW.
  4. Lock screen (optional): long-press the lock screen, Customize, Lock Screen, tap the widget row, Scriptable, then tap it: Script = NQ NOW.

What it shows: the NQ price with ▲ / ▼ vs the last close, each bot as an emoji: 🟢 in trade +$X, 👀 setup 4/10, ⏸️ waiting, 🔴 PC silent; today's total $; ⏳ the next news / half day / roll with a countdown; MFFU's 7-day last day. Tap = the NQ dashboard. BLITZ is never shown.

Honest note: iOS decides when widgets redraw (it asks every 5 min; usually 5-15 min). The PC posts every 15 min. The bottom line always says how old the numbers are: live · 10:26 ET, as of 09:42 ET in gold after 20 min, PC silent in red after an hour.

Siri: "Hey Siri, NQ status"

  1. One tap:
    Paste, name it NQ status, Done. Say "Hey Siri, NQ status".
  2. Again: tap the button once more, paste, name it Why no trade. Say "Hey Siri, why no trade".

NQ status speaks: "NQ 25,665, down 1.3 percent. Zlarior is forming a long setup at stage 4 of 10, ATLAS is waiting, no trades today, zero dollars. Next: the market halts at 1:15." Why no trade speaks the newest setup the bot skipped and why, in plain words.

Same file as the widget; the script's name picks what it says. Old numbers say so ("As of 9:45 ..." / "The PC has been quiet since ...").

The big Olympus widget (all apps + 8 buttons)

  1. Get Scriptable (free). Open the App Store
  2. One tap:
    It copies the script and opens a new blank script. Long-press, Paste, tap the title, name it Olympus, Done.
  3. Add it: long-press the Home Screen, tap + (top left), search Scriptable, swipe to Large (half the screen) or Medium, Add Widget.
  4. Long-press the widget, Edit Widget, Script = Olympus. Done.

Already have the old Olympus script? Replace its text with the new copy: the widget picks it up by itself.

What you get

Top: PC status and today's $ (tap = Board). One line per account: balance, today $, drawdown room left, and LONG / SHORT +$ when a trade is open (tap = NQ bot). Large adds Kalshi and the NQ price.

Buttons (Large = 8, Medium = 4: NQ bot, Kalshi, Jarvis, Decide): each opens its page.

Bottom: where the numbers came from (LIVE · PC, PC · OLD, PAPER · SITE, CACHED) and their age.

Honest note on refresh: iOS decides when widgets redraw, usually every 5-15 min (it asks every 15 min). Numbers are never newer than the PC's last 15-min post: check the age at the bottom. Buttons open Safari or your Home Screen app. Read only: it sends nothing and cannot arm or place anything.
