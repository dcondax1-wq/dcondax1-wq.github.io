3 steps (2 minutes)

  1. Get Scriptable (free, App Store, by Simon Støvring). Open the App Store
  2. Copy the script with the button below. In Scriptable tap +, paste, tap the title and name it Olympus, tap Done.
  3. Add the widget: long-press the Home Screen, tap + (top left), find Scriptable, pick the Large size (or Small / Medium), Add Widget. Long-press it, Edit Widget, Script = Olympus.
Download

Optional: in Edit Widget, Parameter light or dark fixes the look; small / medium / large forces a layout. It refreshes about every 15 minutes (iOS decides the exact time). Tap it to open the site.

What it shows

Each account: balance, today's $, $ to pass (or $ to payout once funded), drawdown room. PC status, the next Kalshi clock window, when it last refreshed. A LIVE · PC tag means the numbers came from your PC's 15-minute post; PAPER · SITE = the site copy; CACHED = no signal, the last good copy with its age.

Read only: it reads the same public numbers the site shows, sends nothing, holds no password or key and cannot place or arm anything.
