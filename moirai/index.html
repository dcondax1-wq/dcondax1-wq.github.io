    Moirai is a simulation, not a forecast. It plays the odds you set 1,000 times with a fixed seed; it never places or changes an order and does not move the bot's size. Every R is shown with its dollars (1R = the risk per trade on the chosen account, never more than its daily loss limit). Modelled from MFFU's rulebook as checked on 2026-10-18: the trailing floor, the soft daily loss limit, a minimum number of trading days and, as a switch, the 50% best-day rule. Not modelled: slippage beyond the costs already in the R's and intraday swings, so a real eval can be slower or worse.
