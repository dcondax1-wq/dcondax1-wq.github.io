                Kalshi words, in plain English
                Contract
                One yes/no ticket. It pays $1 if you are right and $0 if you are wrong.
                Price (¢)
                What one contract costs. 50¢ means the market thinks it is a coin flip.
                Fee
                Kalshi's cut on every buy: biggest near 50¢ (about 1.75¢ a contract), tiny near 99¢.
                Fair / break-even
                The price where a bet neither makes nor loses money after the fee, given how often it wins.
                Win rate
                How often the bet won. It has to beat the break-even rate to make money.
                R
                One unit of risk: what one bet can lose. +2R = made twice the bet.
                EV
                Expected value: what a bet is worth on average, after the fee.
                n
                How many bets a number is based on. Under 30 is mostly luck.
                Paper
                Written down as if bet, with no money. Nothing is bought.
