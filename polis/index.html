        The night sky of myths

        Tonight's sky over New York

          How the sky is drawn

          The stars are real: each one's right ascension and declination (J2000, from the Bright Star catalogue) is turned into height and compass direction for New York (40.71 N, 74.01 W) at this minute, using sidereal time. The Sun and the Moon use the standard almanac formulas. The picture faces south-south-east over the Acropolis; walking the city turns the sky with you (left is east, right is west). A shooting star crosses the sky when today is a new best day.
