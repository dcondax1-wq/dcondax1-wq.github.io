Set up your iPhone

Five short steps so Olympus works like a real app: its own icon, the main apps one tap away, a widget, alerts and Siri.

Step 1 · 1 minute

Olympus on your Home Screen

  1. Open your Olympus link in Safari (not Chrome: only Safari can add it).
  2. Tap Share (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. From now on open Olympus from its icon: it opens full screen, and notifications only work this way.

Done: you are in the Home Screen app right now.

Step 2 · 2 minutes

The main apps as their own icons

Each of these has its own icon and opens straight to itself. For each: tap Copy its link, paste it in Safari, then Share, Add to Home Screen, Add. Put them in one row like a dock.

Step 4 · 1 minute

Notifications: every trade, the result in $

  1. Open Olympus from its Home Screen icon (step 1; iOS 16.4 or later).
  2. On the Jarvis home, tap Allow notifications, then Allow. Within about 5 minutes your PC says "Olympus notifications are on".
  3. Backup (optional): the free ntfy app, subscribed to your alert topic (BOTS_BACK.bat on the PC shows it). An alert goes there only when the phone app did not take it, never twice.

Step 5 · optional

"Hey Siri, open the NQ bot"

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, add the action Open URLs and paste an app's link (step 2: Copy its link).
  2. Name the shortcut the way you will say it, for example NQ bot. Tap Done.
  3. Now say "Hey Siri, NQ bot". Asking Jarvis questions by voice from Siri is not built yet; it is on the Jarvis team's list.
