Emergency

First: an open trade's stop and target sit at the broker. They still work if the PC, NinjaTrader or the internet dies.

1The PC is off or says PC SILENT›
  1. A trade open? Its stop and target still work. To get out now: Tradovate / MFFU app on the phone > the account > Flatten.
  2. At the PC: double-click PC_DOCTOR.bat. It only looks. Read MOST LIKELY CAUSE and run the line under NEXT STEP.
  3. Still silent 20 min later: send Claude a photo of the PC_DOCTOR window. The bot never chases a missed entry.
2A trade is open and the bot looks stuck›

Do nothing first: the stop and target close the trade even if the bot is frozen.

  1. Want out now: your PC link + :8444/safety/ > FLATTEN (tap twice). The bot keeps running, no new entry today.
  2. That page does not open: Tradovate / MFFU app > the account > Flatten.
  3. Never add a stop by hand. Tell Claude what you saw.
3NinjaTrader disconnected›
  1. At the PC: NinjaTrader > Connections > connect again (or close and reopen NinjaTrader).
  2. Keep the MNQ chart with LE_BarFeed open. The bot blocks new entries while bars are old and trades again by itself.
  3. Not back in a few minutes and a trade is open: Tradovate / MFFU app > Flatten.
4An alert says something went wrong›
  1. Read the alert: most say what to do. "FLATTEN BY HAND" = NinjaTrader > Flatten Everything.
  2. At the PC: double-click HEALTH_CHECK.bat. Each red line says its fix.
  3. Not clear: send Claude the alert text. "Entry blocked" = a guard refused an unsafe order: nothing to do.
5An account hit the daily loss limit›

A DLL hit is a pause for today, not a fail.

  1. Nothing to do: orders show Limit 0 and the bot stays off that account by itself.
  2. Check its status on the MFFU dashboard (Active / Failed / Passed).
  3. Do not trade it by hand. It trades again after 18:00 ET.
6An update is blocked›

The old version keeps trading. Nothing is broken.

  1. Phone says "update blocked by a local change in X": send Claude that line. Never edit files in the bot folder.
  2. To try again at the PC after 16:00 ET, bot flat: double-click UPDATE.bat.
  3. [OK] = done. [FIX] = that line says what (for example: a trade is open, run it after it closes).
python -X utf8 -m engine.autoupdate --source-status

At the PC, read only: says whether the PC is connected to your backup repo.

7The phone site shows old numbers›
  1. Look at the top of ZEUS: "PC reported X min ago". Under 30 min = the numbers are live (the PC reports every 15 min).
  2. Red PC SILENT = the PC is quiet, not the site: see card 1.
  3. The page itself looks old: close the Home Screen app fully (swipe it away) and open it again.
8Stop everything right now›
  1. At the PC: http://localhost:8766 > Bot Control > HOLD TO KILL (hold the red button). It flattens every account, cancels orders, stops the bot, auto-resume off.
  2. From the phone: your PC link + :8444/safety/ > KILL (tap twice). The same, and it also stops Kalshi when Kalshi is live.
  3. PC out of reach: Tradovate / MFFU app > Flatten. Starting again is only at the PC, with your live phrase.
python -X utf8 -m engine.phone_safety --on

Once, at the PC: turns the phone KILL / FLATTEN / PAUSE buttons on. Off again: the same line with --off.

9Add an account›
  1. Buy it at MFFU with the CLUB code. Open NinjaTrader: the new account shows in the Accounts tab.
  2. Open http://localhost:8766/add-account/ on the PC (or your PC link + /add-account/ on the phone). Pick the plan, type or tap the Name, type I ACCEPT LIVE RISK.
  3. Tap Add. It starts trading once the bot is flat. Check it at the PC with the line below.
python -X utf8 -m engine.accounts status

One line per account: armed or not, and your MFFU slots.

10Turn Kalshi off›

Kalshi is on paper today: no real money moves until you type the Kalshi phrase.

  1. Live and you want it off: your PC link + :8444/kalshi-live/ > STOP (one tap).
  2. Or phone KILL (card 8): it stops Kalshi too.
  3. At the PC: double-click KALSHI_STOP.bat, type Y. It cancels every resting Kalshi order and switches Kalshi OFF.

"Your PC link" = your PC's private Tailscale address (the one LIVE_LINK.bat showed). Never share it.

Words the pages use

Room
$ between the balance and the account's floor. $0 room = the account fails. The bot sizes every trade inside it.
Drawdown / max loss
How far an account may fall from its best close before it fails: 50K $2,000, 25K $1,000.
Floor
The balance where the account fails (best close minus the max loss). It moves up with your best close.
To pass
$ still needed to pass an eval: 50K +$3,000, 25K +$1,500 from the start.
Eval / funded
Eval = the test you pay for. Funded = passed: real payouts.
DLL
Daily loss limit. MFFU's soft one: hit it and the account pauses for the day, it does not fail. Builders $1,000, Rapid EOD 25K $500. The bot sizes so one full stop stays inside it.
EOD
End of day: a Rapid EOD account measures its max loss from the day's closing balance, not every tick.
Limit 0
MFFU lets the account hold 0 contracts now: 16:10-18:00 ET daily (normal), or it hit its DLL, failed or passed.
R
One unit of risk = what the trade loses at its stop. +2R = won twice the risk.
PF (profit factor)
$ won divided by $ lost. Above 1 makes money; 2 = $2 won per $1 lost.
Win %
Share of trades that made money.
Twin
The same trade in the backtest. Live vs twin shows whether the bot trades as tested.
Edge check / edge light
GREEN = the last 40 sessions look like the tested strategy. AMBER = weaker, watch. RED = advice to size down. It never changes anything by itself.
Circuit breaker
Live trades vs the twin: WATCH, SIZE DOWN or PAUSE advice. Advice only.
Sweep
Price runs past an old high or low (where stops sit), then turns.
Stop / target
The exit if wrong (1 tick past the sweep) and the one exit if right. Both sit at the broker, so they work even if the PC dies.
MNQ / micro
Micro Nasdaq future: $2 per point.
Flat
No open trade.
Armed
A real account switched on to trade. Only you arm it, by typing I ACCEPT LIVE RISK.
Paper / Sim
Pretend trades, no money.
PC SILENT
The PC has not reported for 30+ minutes.
Kill switch
Close everything, cancel orders, stop the bot, auto-resume off: card 8.
Decide card
A yes / no question for you on the Board, with the $ impact.
ZEUS Lab
Ideas tested every night. Nothing changes without your yes.
