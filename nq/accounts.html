New account? On the PC: TRADE_HOME, choose 4 (Add an account), then START BOT and type your phrase. It shows up here by itself with its 7-day clock; the phrase turns its keep-alive on.

MFFU closes an eval or funded account with no filled trade in 7 calendar days, weekends included. The keep-alive places 1 MNQ in and out (about 2 minutes) on the last full session before that, only if nothing else traded.

Funded accounts show MFFU's way: profit from $0. Payout dates are estimates from 1,000 replays, not a promise. Read only: this page never places, arms or changes anything.
