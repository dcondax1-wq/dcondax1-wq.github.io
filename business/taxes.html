Taxes

US federal rules for 2026, in plain words

In one minute

NOT ADVICE
  • MFFU payouts are pay for a service, so they are self-employment income. Nobody takes tax out for you.
  • You pay income tax plus 15.3% self-employment tax on 92.35% of your profit (about 14% of profit).
  • Eval fees, resets, data, platform and a business share of the PC and internet lower the profit.
  • Pay four times a year. Next date: Jan 15, 2027.
  • The rule: move 30% of every payout to a separate tax pot the day it lands.

How your payouts are taxed

The form
A 1099 (normally 1099-NEC) in late January or February. Paid through Rise: Rise mails it. Paid by bank (Plaid/ACH): MFFU mails it.
No form?
From 2026 a firm only has to send one at $2,000 or more. The money is taxable either way.
Where it goes
Schedule C (profit or loss) and Schedule SE (self-employment tax) with your Form 1040.
SE tax
15.3% = 12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare, on 92.35% of profit. Half of it is deducted from your income.
Not 60/40
The futures 60/40 rule is for your own contracts. A payout is the firm paying you, so treat it as normal income.
QBI
Likely up to 20% of the profit off your income tax (Form 8995). It does not lower SE tax.
Losing year
If fees beat payouts and you run it like a business, the loss can lower other income. Ask a pro.

What you can deduct

  • Eval fees, resets, activation fees (Builder 50K $91.80, 25K $63 / $43). Keep each receipt email.
  • Market data, NinjaTrader, VPS and other trading tools.
  • The PC: the business share. Up to $2,500 an item can usually be written off at once.
  • Internet and phone: the business share only (say 40%). Write down how you picked it.
  • Home office: only a space used regularly and only for the business. Simple way: $5 a square foot, up to 300 sq ft ($1,500).
  • Agency costs if you start it: domain, hosting, software, ads.

Not deductible: personal costs and the private share of anything.

Quarterly dates

1040-ES
  1. Sep - Dec 2026Jan 15, 2027
  2. Jan - Mar 2027Apr 15, 2027
  3. Apr - May 2027Jun 15, 2027
  4. Jun - Aug 2027Sep 15, 2027
  • Needed if you expect to owe $1,000 or more for the year.
  • No penalty if on time you paid 90% of this year's tax, or 100% of last year's (110% if last year's income was over $150,000).
  • Pay free at IRS Direct Pay or your IRS online account: pick "estimated tax, 1040-ES" and the right year.

A date on a weekend or holiday moves to the next business day. 2027 dates follow the usual IRS schedule; check the 2027 Form 1040-ES.

At $2,800 a month: $840 to the pot, $1,960 yours, about $2,520 ready each quarter.

Why 30%: on $2,800 a month with no other job the federal tax is roughly 18%; with a day job it is closer to 30%, and most states add more. 30% covers both. Leftover pot money after you file is yours.

  • Kalshi sends a 1099-INT (interest), 1099-MISC (rewards), 1099-B or 1099-DA only for some items. Your trading profit is usually not on a form.
  • Download the yearly profit and loss file (Account, Tax Info / Documents) and keep it.

  • It is separate from the MFFU income.

Your state

Most states tax this too and many want their own quarterly payments. No personal income tax: Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington, Wyoming (New Hampshire and Washington can still tax business receipts above a size). Check your state's revenue website.

Records to keep

  • Every payout: date, account, amount, the Rise or bank statement.
  • Every fee, reset, data and platform receipt.
  • PC receipt, internet bills and the business % you used; home office size.
  • Your 1099s and each estimated payment's confirmation number.
  • Last year's return (for the no-penalty rule) and the Kalshi yearly file.

Keep them at least 3 years after you file. The Ledger app already holds your fees and payouts.

Get help free

  • VITA: free IRS volunteers, usually for income around $70,000 or less. Some do not take business returns with many costs, so ask when you book. Find one with the VITA locator on IRS.gov.
  • IRS Free File: free guided software if your income qualifies.
  • Above that, one visit a year to a tax preparer or enrolled agent is usually worth it for a business return with quarterly payments.
