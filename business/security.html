The one rule

Never type a password or a login code into a chat, an email or a call. Claude never needs your passwords. Nobody from MFFU, your bank or Apple will ask for one.

This page only keeps your ticks, on this phone. It never asks for a password.

4. Recovery codes, offline

0 of 1
  • Not in photos, not in email, not in notes on the phone. They get you back in if you lose the phone.

5. A PIN on your phone carrier

If you think you were hacked

  1. From a safe device, change your email password first. Then "log out of all devices".
  2. Change the bank, MFFU and broker passwords. Check 2-step login is still on.
  3. Money moved? Call your bank now and freeze the cards.
  4. Tell MFFU support, so they can lock the account.
  5. Phone suddenly has no signal? Call your carrier: it may be a SIM swap.
  6. PC looks wrong? Press STOP BOT, then run a virus scan.
  7. Freeze your credit for free at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion.
  8. Report it for free at identitytheft.gov (US).
