Type the prospect's name: the demo opens with their name, town and number in it. Nothing is saved or sent; it is all in the link.

How to use it in a pitch

  1. Open the demo closest to their trade before you walk in (it loads in about a second on 4G).
  2. Hand them the phone on the 3D part: "drag it". Let them play for 10 seconds; say nothing.
  3. Tap "Book" so they see the request land, then: "This, with your name, your photos, in two weeks."
