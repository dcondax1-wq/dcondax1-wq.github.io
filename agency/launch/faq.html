FAQ

Straight answers

Why did you build a site for my business without asking?

Because it is easier to decide when you can see it. It costs you nothing and nothing goes live without your yes. If you don't want it, tell us and we take it down.

Where did you get my business details?

From public map listings (OpenStreetMap): your name, phone, address and hours as they are listed there. If something is wrong, we fix it.

Who owns the domain and the site?

You do. The domain is bought in your name. Once paid in full, your words, photos and logo are yours.

How fast can it go live?

The sample is already built. After your yes and your photos and words, the finished site can be live within 24 hours on a free address, and on your own domain as soon as it is connected (usually the same day).

What does the care plan cover?

Hosting, security updates, keeping your hours and details current, and small edits each month.

Can I stop the monthly plan?

Yes, with 30 days notice. The Call engine has a 3-month minimum. If you stop, we hand the site over or switch it off after 30 days.

Do you write fake reviews or buy ads for me?

No. We never write reviews. The Call engine sends your real customers a polite review request that you approve.

Do you call businesses with robocalls?

No. We never use auto-dialers or recorded calls. Calls to our number are answered by our assistant, which takes a message for a person.
