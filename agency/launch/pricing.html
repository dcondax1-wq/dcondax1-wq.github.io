Terms

50 % deposit to start

Work starts when the deposit is paid. The other half is due the day the site goes live, before it is switched on.

What is included is written down

The proposal lists the pages and features. Anything new (an extra page, a shop, a new 3D model) is quoted first, in writing, before any work.

Two rounds of changes

Two rounds of changes are included before launch. More rounds are billed by the hour at your rate, agreed first.

Client sends photos, logo and words

The clock starts when the client sends their photos, logo and words (or buys the writing / photo extras). Late content moves the date, not the price.

Hosting and the domain

The domain and the Google profile stay in the client's name. Hosting is part of the monthly plan; if they stop paying, the site is handed over or switched off after 30 days notice.

Who owns what

Once paid in full, the client owns their words, photos, logo and domain. You keep the right to reuse your own code and templates and to show the site in your portfolio.

Care plan: 12-month term, then 30 days notice

The care plan ($79 a month) starts on launch day and runs for a 12-month term, billed on the same day each month; after that it continues month to month and either side can stop it with 30 days notice. The Call engine has a 3-month minimum (reviews take time to add up).

Late payment

An invoice is due in 14 days. After 30 days late, edits pause until it is paid.

Logins stay with the client

The client keeps the passwords for their domain and Google account; you get your own login, never theirs.

Written OK before any 3D scan

Get the owner's written permission before you scan a space or its products, and say where the tour will be shown.
