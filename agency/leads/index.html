    How this list is made

    • OpenStreetMap (the free world map) lists shops, restaurants, trades and offices with their tags. The list keeps the ones with no website tag (a Facebook page alone counts as no real site) and leaves out chains.
    • Hot = no site + a phone + opening hours + a street address (a real, active business). Trades, health and auto score a bit higher: their customers search online.
    • The map can be out of date: look the business up before you call. Business lines only, inside the legal hours (the call app checks the hours and Do Not Call for you).
    • New towns: on the PC, python tools/web_leads.py --town "Frederick" (or --town "Bel Air" --bbox 39.50,-76.38,39.57,-76.31 for any town).
