Website checks

A one-page report you can send a business owner: how their home page does on a phone, the 3 things that lose them customers (with the source of every number), and what is missing. The two samples are big brands, so no small business is named here.

  • Starbucks Coffee Company D2026-11-20 · main content 8.1 s · jumps 0.24 · access 100/100
  • IKEA D2026-11-20 · main content 35.0 s · jumps 0.00 · access 100/100

Run one (on the PC or here)

python -X utf8 tools/site_audit.py https://their-site.com

Add --visitors 1500 --value 120 for a $ estimate (their phone visitors a month, what a new customer is worth). The report lands in tools/web_public/agency/audit/<name>.html with its screenshot; send the link or the file. It reads robots.txt, opens the one home page once, and never contacts anyone.
