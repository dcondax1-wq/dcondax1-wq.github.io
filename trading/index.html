      Why was my order rejected?

      "Max order quantity, Limit 0, Scope MNQ" on MFFU / Tradovate means: this account may hold 0 contracts right now. It is not a bug in the bot. Usually one of three things:

      1. After the 16:10 ET flat time. From 16:10 to 18:00 ET (and from Friday 16:10 to Sunday 18:00 ET) every account is set to 0. Try again after 18:00 ET.
      2. The daily loss limit was hit. The account is blocked until the next session opens at 18:00 ET.
      3. The account failed (max loss) or passed. A failed eval stays at 0. A passed eval waits at 0 until the funded account is activated.

      How to tell which: if it is between 16:10 and 18:00 ET, wait for 18:00 ET and try again. If it still says Limit 0 after 18:00 ET, open the MFFU dashboard and read the account's status (Active, Failed, Passed or Pending activation).

      Trading reads the site's own published files and sends nothing. It cannot start, stop or change a bot, and it never places an order.
